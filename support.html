Help and support

How can we help?

Quick answers about MoneyBias and the money tools. Still stuck? Email us.

Contact us

Email support@syluneo.com. Tell us which tool you were using, what you expected, and your phone or browser. Please do not include card numbers, bank details or statements; we never need them. We usually reply within a few working days.

Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up or login anywhere on Syluneo. Open a tool and start.

Is it free?

Yes. The quiz and all six tools are free, with no subscription, in-app purchases or ads.

Where do my numbers go?

Nowhere. Your calculations happen in your browser, and we do not store your financial inputs. Refresh the page and they are gone. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Why can't I find my old result?

Because nothing is saved, results are not kept between visits. Download your result card or copy your share link if you want to keep it.

The credit card or EMI result is different from my bank's.

Banks differ in how they calculate minimum due, interest, fees and rounding. Each tool shows its assumptions under “How this is calculated”. Try changing the minimum-due rule or rate to match your statement, and treat your bank's statement as the final word.

Is my quiz result accurate?

The quiz is a fun self-reflection game based on behavioural-finance research, not a validated psychological test. Use it as a starting point for thinking about your habits.

Is this financial advice?

No. The tools give estimates for learning and planning. Syluneo is not registered with SEBI as an investment adviser. Check important decisions with your bank or a qualified adviser.

Is there a mobile app?

Not right now. Syluneo works in any phone browser. You can add it to your home screen from your browser's menu.

I found a calculation mistake.

Thank you! Email the numbers you entered and the result you saw to support@syluneo.com and we will check it.
