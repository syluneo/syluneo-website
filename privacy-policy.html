Privacy Policy

Syluneo is built so that we never see the numbers you type. This page explains exactly what that means.

The short version
  • No account needed. There is no sign-up or login.
  • Your calculations happen in your browser. What you type into the quiz or tools is not sent to us.
  • We do not store your financial inputs. We use no cookies and no advertising trackers.
  • We count visits as anonymous totals with GoatCounter, which never receives what you type.
  • If you email us, we receive your email address and message.

1. Who we are

Syluneo (“Syluneo”, “we”, “us”) runs the website syluneo.com, including MoneyBias, a free money-personality quiz with six money calculators. Contact: support@syluneo.com, Quazipura, Ballia, Uttar Pradesh 277001, India.

2. What happens to what you type

The quiz answers, prices, incomes, card balances, EMI details and other numbers you enter are processed only by code running in your own browser. They are not sent to our server or to anyone else, and they are not saved in cookies, local storage or any database. When you close or refresh the page, they are gone.

If you choose to download a result image, it is created on your device and saved to your device. If you choose to share a link, the link may include your quiz result type (for example, which money type you got) so your friend can see it. It never includes amounts you typed. You decide whether to share it and with whom.

3. Information we do not collect

  • We do not ask for your name, phone number, email address, date of birth or location to use the site.
  • We do not collect bank, card or account details. Syluneo never asks for card numbers, passwords or OTPs. If anyone claiming to be Syluneo asks for these, it is not us.
  • We do not use cookies, advertising pixels, social media plugins or embedded third-party content.
  • The pages load their fonts, images and scripts from syluneo.com itself.

4. Technical logs kept by our hosting provider

Like almost every website, the company that hosts syluneo.com may automatically record standard technical information when your browser requests a page, such as your IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time. This is used to deliver the site, keep it secure and fix problems. These logs do not contain anything you type into the quiz or tools, because that never leaves your browser. We do not use these logs to identify you or build a profile of you, and we do not sell them.

5. Anonymous visit counting

To know how many people use Syluneo, we use GoatCounter, an open-source, privacy-focused visit counter. When a page or app screen opens, your browser sends GoatCounter a short note containing: the page or screen name (for example “No-cost EMI check”), the website you came from, your screen width, and your browser and device type. In the MoneyBias quiz it also notes that a quiz was started or finished and which money type the result was, so we can see totals such as how many people got each type.

GoatCounter never receives your quiz answers or any number you type. According to GoatCounter's privacy policy, it uses no cookies or browser storage on your device, does not store IP addresses or the full browser identifier, and keeps only combined totals per day or hour. Your IP address is used briefly to estimate your country and avoid counting the same visit twice, and is held in memory for at most 8 hours. We see only those totals and cannot identify you from them.

6. If you contact us

If you email support@syluneo.com, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply and to improve the site, and we delete it when it is no longer needed for that, normally within 12 months. Please do not send us bank statements, card numbers or other sensitive financial details; we do not need them to help you.

7. Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. Apart from our hosting provider and GoatCounter, which process only the technical information described above, we share nothing, and we only disclose information if the law requires it.

8. Children

Syluneo is meant for general audiences, especially students and young adults. Because we do not collect the information you enter, we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you are under 18, please use the tools with a parent or guardian.

9. Your rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you (in practice, only emails you have sent us), and ask us to correct or delete it, by writing to support@syluneo.com. We aim to follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and its rules as they apply to us. If you are not satisfied with our reply, you may have the right to complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change how Syluneo handles information, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect. If we ever add another service, we will describe it here first, and nothing will ever receive what you type.
