Questions people ask

Is Syluneo free?

Yes. The quiz and all six tools are free. There is no subscription, no in-app purchase and no ads.

Do I need to sign up?

No. There is no account or login anywhere. Open the quiz or a tool and start.

Do you save my numbers?

No. Your calculations happen in your browser and nothing you type is sent to us. Refresh the page and it is gone.

Is the quiz a real personality test?

It is a fun self-reflection game inspired by behavioural-finance research, not a validated psychological test. Treat your type as a starting point, not a label.

Is this financial advice?

No. The tools give estimates for learning and planning. Syluneo is not registered with SEBI as an investment adviser, so check big decisions with your bank or a qualified adviser.

Why might my bank's numbers differ?

Banks use their own rules for minimum due, interest, fees and rounding. Every tool shows how it calculates, so you can match it to your statement.
